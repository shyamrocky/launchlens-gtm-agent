## Source of truth and expected behavior

Join on (Product ID, Market ID), never country display strings, fuzzy search or
Notion relations. Calendar owns planned dates/milestone timing; readiness owns
confirmation/permission statuses and missing conditions. PRD REQ-007 requires
BOTH datasets for availability per market/date. A public rollout announcement or
planned date alone cannot override missing or pending scenario evidence.

For this frozen pedagogical fixture, all four readiness areas are required. A
missing record/status/evidence is unresolved, never implicitly approved. Pending/
Blocked communication authorization prevents external promotional use even if
other checks pass. Missing availability needs clarification and withholding of
availability claims. All checks Approved supports full-content preparation with
source limitations retained. Content preparation is distinct from actual release:
no real marketing is sent/published, and future-date planning must not claim current
availability. Evaluation date remains explicit; do not call US available before
its planned date, or infer inventory merely because the date has passed.

Scenario test matrix belongs ONLY in m1.4-validation-report.md. Operational CSVs
contain no Proceed / Clarify / Hold column or authoritative derived decision.
Base test dates are each market's own launch day; readiness snapshots precede
those dates by seven days. These are separate case snapshots, not a chronological
approval history. No expiry or ongoing freshness behavior is implemented.
