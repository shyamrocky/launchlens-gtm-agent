## Decision and boundaries

Decision ID: M15B2-001. Status: implementation choice within the owner-approved
scope; results await owner review. Use the accepted authored block boundaries to
make one primary chunk for each complete claim/requirement, plus coherent context
and reference sections. Attach required context and source evidence without
paraphrasing. Rationale: two short, known documents already have validated complete
statements and authoring blocks; a general splitter adds no necessary capability.

inferred paragraph reconstruction and LLM summaries. No new dependency added.

pypdf; normalization/standardization use custom Python; schemas document the format
and explicit Python checks enforce the bounded contract. No Pydantic, LangChain,
tokenizer, OCR, additional parser or embedding model is introduced in this stage.

## Chunking rules and result

| Group | Chunks | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Public product claims | 15 | One complete PROD statement each, including its inline scope |
| Synthetic requirements | 7 | One complete REQ requirement and acceptance criterion each |
| Context, qualifications and references | 23 | Keep author-defined sections/source entries intact |
| Total | 45 | 30 Product Brief + 15 PRD records |

All **96 authored display blocks** occur exactly once as primary content.
Supporting context is intentionally repeated across packets. No blind overlap
window, token split, truncation or summarization is used. Repeated PDF page
headers/footers do not become standalone chunks; the accepted page records still
preserve their extraction. Source-body draft labels remain unchanged.

Each record has a focused primary_text and a complete text evidence packet.
segments retain exact normalized-page substrings, roles, page record IDs, block
indexes and start/end offsets. The full text joins these verbatim segments and
adds clearly labeled original CSV scope/limitation fields and PDF hyperlink
destinations. CSV enrichment is identified separately from PDF text. No new product
claim, source fact, simulated approval, market schedule or marketing copy is created.

- Brief packets carry the document classification/temporal boundary, claim-status
  meaning, shared Mandatory qualification, unknowns, source-date caveats and
  independent-use boundary. Claims additionally carry their exact CSV rows and
  relevant complete S1-S5 source entries, including titles/URLs/publication caveats.
- S1/S4 support the shared rollout qualification. Those references are labeled
  shared_qualification, separately from claim_support; this does not create extra
  claim-source mappings or imply S4 supports every claim.
- PRD packets retain synthetic authority, untested-intent qualifications, scope,
  dependencies and unresolved planning information. Explicit PROD references link
  to product-claim chunk IDs; no Product Brief passage is relabeled as PRD intent
  or merged into a synthetic page segment. Reference ranges are expanded.
- The competitive-context chunk retains both original visible citation labels and
  their two PDF annotation destinations. Public competitor citations do not make
  the PRD's proposed requirements verified capabilities.

Records carry product/document identity, original version/hash/URL/path, all
contributing PDF page numbers, source/preparation acceptance evidence and unique
stable chunk IDs. IDs bind the policy version and full payload before related IDs
are resolved, avoiding circular references; regeneration is byte deterministic.
This is a nested local format, not a finalized Pinecone upload schema.
