## Usage and an important efficiency limitation

Actual hosted usage:188,689 input tokens +136 output tokens =188,825 Nebius tokens;
44 Pinecone query-embedding tokens. Largest model input:102,208 tokens; largest
request:357,228 bytes, below the750,000-byte no-truncation bound. Dollar charges
were not retrieved from billing and are not claimed.

The simple adapter sent full PMM content and evidence packets through native tool
history. That preserved fidelity but repeatedly resent substantial context. Focused
embedding inputs reduce vectorization cost; they do not automatically reduce the
chat-model context. This is a concrete efficiency limitation, not a retrieval miss.
A byte cap is not a tokenizer-based context-fit guarantee for every future run.

Recommendation before broader live usage: review a small deterministic model-context
projection that preserves relevant scope, citations, qualifications, authority and
partial-evidence warnings, while keeping full packets in authoritative run state.
Do not rebuild chunks, change the frozen corpus, silently trim evidence, introduce
another database or claim savings before testing. This adjustment and any further
live validation require the owner's next bounded approval.
