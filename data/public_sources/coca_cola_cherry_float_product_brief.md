## B. Product overview

The documented product is a flavored soda with cherry and vanilla notes, inspired by classic soda floats (PROD-001 to PROD-003). These are publisher-supported descriptions, not taste-test findings. Diet Coke Cherry is a separate product and must not be treated as the Zero Sugar Cherry Float variant.
